This Privacy Policy explains how information submitted through this website and app is collected and used. It is provided for general information and does not constitute legal advice.

## Information we collect

Depending on how you use this website, we may collect: (a) your name, email address, phone number and enquiry details when you submit a contact or appointment request; (b) your name, email address and password when you create an account for a monitoring subscription; (c) financial and business data you voluntarily upload to use a monitoring product (e.g. monthly financial figures, loan details, receivables/payables); and (d) payment details processed for a subscription — we never see or store your card, UPI, or bank details directly, as payments are handled entirely by Razorpay.

## How we use it

Information submitted is used to respond to your enquiry, provide the professional services or monitoring tools you request, process payments for subscriptions, and send transactional emails (such as account confirmation or payment receipts). It is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Third-party services we use

We use Supabase for secure data storage and account authentication, Razorpay for payment processing (Razorpay's own privacy policy governs how they handle your payment details), and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this website, which uses cookies to collect anonymised usage data.

## Data storage

Submitted information is stored securely using Supabase infrastructure with access restricted to authorised administrators through database-level access controls.

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have submitted — including closing your account and deleting your data — by contacting us through the contact page.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the website or app constitutes acceptance of the current version.
