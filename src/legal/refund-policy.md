This policy applies to consultation booking fees and monthly monitoring subscriptions paid on this website through Razorpay.

## Consultation booking fee

The consultation fee is ₹1,000 (inclusive of GST) per session and is payable in advance to reserve a consultation.

- **Rescheduling:** You may reschedule a confirmed consultation free of charge by informing us at least 24 hours before the scheduled time.
- **Cancellation by you:** If you cancel at least 24 hours before the scheduled time, the fee is refunded in full. Cancellations within 24 hours, or not attending without notice, are not eligible for a refund.
- **Cancellation by us:** If we are unable to offer a suitable slot within 7 days of your payment, or we cancel a confirmed consultation, you may choose a new slot or receive a full refund.
- **Out-of-scope requests:** If, on review, your requirement falls outside the services we provide, we will inform you before the consultation and refund the fee in full.

## Monthly monitoring subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed monthly. You can cancel at any time before your next billing date to stop future charges. Amounts already paid for the current month are generally not refundable, except where required by law or where we are unable to provide the service.

## How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through Razorpay within 5–7 working days of approval. The time taken for the amount to reflect in your account depends on your bank or card issuer.

## How to request a refund or reschedule

Reply to your booking confirmation email or contact us through the contact page, quoting your booking reference (for example SKB-XXXXXXXX) and Razorpay payment ID.

## Delivery of services

Consultations are delivered by video call, phone or in person as agreed. No physical goods are shipped.
